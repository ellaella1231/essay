Grade 7 English Essay Rubric:

1. Vocabulary: Expects use of basic adjectives and adverbs. Look for variety beyond "good" or "bad".
2. Grammar: Correct use of present simple, past simple, and future (will/going to). Basic subject-verb agreement is mandatory.
3. Structure: A clear beginning, middle, and end. Use of basic linking words (and, but, because, so).
4. Content: Should stay on topic and provide simple reasons or examples.

When correcting mistakes, provide suggestions that fit a 7th-grade level. Do not use overly complex university-level vocabulary. Maintain the student's original intent.
